02 / Research

Published peer-reviewed work

Interpretability research on where neural networks actually draw their lines — and why that geometry matters for trust and robustness.

★ IEEE IJCNN 2025 · First Author

SigmaCam — Exact Decision Boundary Extraction for DNNs with Smooth Nonlinearities

Tools like SplineCam recover exact decision boundaries only for piecewise-linear (ReLU) networks — they break on the smooth activations that dominate modern architectures. SigmaCam closes that gap with a theoretically exact, recursive level-set algorithm that traces the true boundary of MLPs using Sigmoid and SiLU, rather than approximating it by sampling.
